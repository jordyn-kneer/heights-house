Why we built it

A place to bring the Heights together.

Heights House is a home base in the middle of Peoria Heights. It's a place to work, meet, learn and hang out, built for people who would rather do business around good people than alone at the kitchen table.

At our core, we're real estate agents, and we wanted a coworking space that feels open to everyone, where your brokerage doesn't matter. Come learn with us, share what's working, and lean on each other when a deal gets complicated.

And it's bigger than real estate. We want entrepreneurs and local business owners swapping ideas over coffee, learning from each other, and showing up for lunches out of the garage in the summer and Christmas parties in the winter.

Come for the desk. Stay for the people.

Right in the middle of it all

Peoria Heights puts you a short drive from showings all over the Peoria area, with coffee and lunch spots around the corner for the time between appointments.

Somewhere to keep the signs

Sign, rider and open house kit storage in the garage, so your trunk and your garage at home get a break.

A room that wins listings

Sit sellers down in a real office with the smart board for your listing presentation, not at a coffee shop table.

A studio for your content

Record market updates, listing walkthroughs and client stories in the podcast room.

Trainings that pay for themselves

Hands-on sessions on contracts, listings, marketing and tech, taught by people doing the work.

Someone to call when a deal gets weird

Agents from different brokerages under one roof. Somebody down the hall has seen it before.

Questions

The stuff agents always ask

Do I have to switch brokerages?

No. Heights House is open to agents from every brokerage. Your membership is with the house, not a team, and nobody's going to pitch you.

Do you take a cut of my deals?

No. You pay a flat monthly membership, and that's it. Your commission stays between you and your brokerage.

Can I bring clients?

Yes. Meet them at your desk, or book the conference room for consults, signings and listing presentations.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Memberships are month to month. Just let us know before your next month starts.

Is it only for real estate agents?

Agents are the heart of the house, but lenders, title, insurance, photographers and local business owners are welcome too.

Where do I park?

A new lot is being built out back. You can also park on the street or at Heritage Square.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Your first month is free. Come check it out.
